# Green Hospitality Signs

Welcome to the product guide for **Green Hospitality Signs**. Every sign is a physical part with a SurfaceGui — there's nothing to configure or set up, they simply work as soon as they're placed.

## Products

- 🚫 **Staff Only** - Restricts access to authorized personnel.
- 🚫 **No Glassware in Kitchen** - Prevents glass hazards in food prep areas.
- 🔥 **Fire Blanket** - Marks the location of the fire blanket.
- ⚠️ **Danger: Deep Fryer** - Warns of hot oil and deep fryer hazards.
- ⚠️ **All Food Must Be Covered and Dated** - Ensures proper food storage and rotation.
- 🧼 **Employees Must Wash Hands** - Reminds staff of hygiene requirements.
- 🧼 **Wash Hands Before Returning to Work** - Reminds staff of hygiene requirements upon returning to the station.
- 🚭 **No Smoking, Vaping, or E-Cigarettes** - Enforces a smoke-free environment.
- ⚠️ **Danger: Hot Surface** - Warns of potential burn hazards.
- ➕ **First Aid Kit** - Marks the location of the first aid kit.
- 🏃 **Fire Exit Keep Clear** - Ensures fire exits remain unobstructed.
- ⚠️ **Food Prep Area** - Keeps food preparation zones clean and free of personal items.
